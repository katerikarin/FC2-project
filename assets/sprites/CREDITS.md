# Explorer artwork

The two cave explorers are original pixel art drawn for Ruins of the Unown with Codex assistance. Explorer A wears a brass lamp and a green field jacket. Explorer B wears a teal hood, coral scarf and blue coat. Both sheets were drawn on a blank grid. The FireRed/LeafGreen reference sheets remain in the local course folder.

Each transparent PNG is 96 × 112 pixels. Each cell is 24 × 28 pixels. From top to bottom the rows face down, left, right and up. From left to right the columns show idle, a second idle pose, left step and right step. The game loads the two sheets at `:/sprites/explorer_a.png` and `:/sprites/explorer_b.png`.
